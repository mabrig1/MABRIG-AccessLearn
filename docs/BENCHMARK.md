# MABRIG AccessLearn: competitors and differentiated position (19 October 2026)

This is a product-research comparison, not direct usability-test evidence.

| Product | Verified strength | Gap AccessLearn may test, not assume | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kolibri (Learning Equality) | Offline-first teaching, content libraries, local classroom server | Low-friction conversion of local Nigerian teacher handouts into accessibility-checked packs | https://learningequality.org/kolibri/about-kolibri/ |
| Microsoft Immersive Reader | Read aloud, text spacing, reading focus and picture dictionary | Nigerian school adoption workflow, pilot reports and teacher approvals | https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/education/student-help-center/boost-reading-comprehension-with-learning-tools |
| Bookshare Plus (Benetech) | Converts inaccessible PDFs and handouts into accessible formats | Localised mobile and low-bandwidth service design; cannot claim unique PDF conversion | https://www.bookshare.org/plus |
| ReadSpeaker TextAid | OCR, read-aloud, file library, dyslexia aids, LMS integrations, offline audio exports | Local Nigerian pilot and educator-driven evidence workflow; competitor has advanced assistive features | https://www.readspeaker.com/products/textaid/ |
| Google Read Along | Offline after first download, speech feedback, reading coaching | University handouts and teacher-authored instructional packs | https://support.google.com/readalong/answer/12279465?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en |
| Efiwe | Mobile offline coding, multilingual model and validated Fellowship selection | Cross-subject adaptations and evidence audits, not coding teaching | https://efiwe.com/blog/efiwe-selected-for-the-2026-mastercard-foundation-edtech-fellowship/ |

## Proposed differentiator

**Teacher-controlled, source-grounded accessibility packaging for Nigerian handouts on low-cost devices**, with explicit offline fallback and an ethics-ready evaluation plan. This is a hypothesis until educators and students validate it.

## Realistic agent roles

1. Intake & permissions (privacy gate).
2. Curriculum planner (AI cloud only).
3. Accessibility adapter (AI cloud only).
4. Assessment builder (AI cloud only).
5. Evidence QA (deterministic source quote checks).
6. Teacher approval (manual).
7. Pilot metrics (local demo only; consent-based institutional data is future work).

## Limitations and safeguards

- No automatic sign-language avatar, OCR of scanned PDFs, or real-time voice recognition.
- Browser text-to-speech may not work offline on all phones. It does not create an MP3.
- Quotes matching source are NOT proof that a paraphrase is pedagogically correct; human review is mandatory.
- Offline preparation is a structural preview, not AI simplification.
- Stronger accessible formats and WCAG 2.2 AA compliance require professional testing, including disabled learners.
- Do not send private student materials to AI providers without lawful basis, policy review and explicit authorisation.
